# Checklist

Review the active specification for completeness and create a checklist under its `checklists/` directory.

Verify at minimum:
- user value and scope are clear;
- requirements are testable and unambiguous;
- acceptance scenarios and edge cases exist;
- success criteria are measurable;
- security/privacy requirements are covered where relevant;
- assumptions and dependencies are documented;
- implementation details have not leaked into the product specification.

Do not treat a checklist as a substitute for tests or implementation verification.
