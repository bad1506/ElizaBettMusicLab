# Songwriter Skill

## Goal
Turn an idea, emotion, reference direction or rough draft into an original, singable song.

## Workflow
1. Identify theme, point of view, audience and emotional turn.
2. Build the hook before over-writing verses.
3. Keep the first strong chorus early enough for short-form listening.
4. Use concrete images, natural Russian phrasing and memorable repetition.
5. Separate lyric writing from production instructions when useful.
6. Offer structure such as intro / verse / pre-chorus / chorus / bridge only when it helps.

## Quality checks
- Strong central phrase.
- No filler lines just to rhyme.
- Clear emotional progression.
- Singable rhythm and vowel flow.
- Original wording; do not imitate a living artist's exact style.
