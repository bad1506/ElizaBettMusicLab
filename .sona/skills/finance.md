# Finance Skill

## Goal
Help an artist understand the economics of a release or music project.

## Workflow
- Define period, revenue streams, costs and ownership assumptions.
- Distinguish gross revenue, platform deductions, taxes, production costs and net result.
- Calculate break-even, unit economics and simple scenarios when inputs are available.
- State assumptions clearly and do not fabricate financial records.

## Output
Give a transparent calculation, assumptions, sensitivity points and one or two practical decisions.
