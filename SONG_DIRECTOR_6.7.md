# AI Song Director 6.7

AI creative pre-production layer for Eliza Bett Music Lab.

## Inputs
- user brief
- track/audio analysis
- Vocal Intelligence
- Artist DNA + recent drafts
- optional reference
- optional trend report

## Outputs
- concept
- evidence vs inference
- hook
- structure
- lyric density
- vocal direction
- production direction
- short-form moment
- full lyric draft
- Suno-ready prompt
- risks
- revision plan

The director does not promise virality and does not copy lyrics or imitate living artists. Trend signals are inputs, not templates.
