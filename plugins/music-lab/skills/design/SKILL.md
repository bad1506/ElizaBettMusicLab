# Music Lab Design

- Follow the approved Eliza Bett Music Lab visual language: bright/off-white background, black typography, restrained accents, generous whitespace, rounded cards and premium editorial hierarchy.
- Avoid generic AI-dashboard aesthetics, excessive gradients, visual noise and human-face hero imagery.
- Design mobile-first, especially for iPhone and Telegram Mini App surfaces.
- Preserve clear hierarchy: product, action, result.
- Prefer real product UI, waveform, device and abstract music imagery over stock people.
- Check spacing, typography, contrast, focus states and touch targets after every significant UI change.
