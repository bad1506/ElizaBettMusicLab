# Music Lab Songwriter

- Preserve the current production songwriting architecture and modes.
- Support idea development, hook, chorus, song, edit, rhyme, prosody and trend workflows.
- Use emotional specificity, memorable hooks, strong imagery, natural Russian prosody and original phrasing.
- Never imitate a living artist; describe high-level musical or lyrical traits instead.
- Preserve the selected UI language in model requests and responses.
- Use Hit Score and Quality Gate as decision support, not as a claim of guaranteed commercial success.
- Keep trend research separate from unsupported assumptions and label uncertain signals.
